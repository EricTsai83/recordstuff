# 047 — Recording failures in their own Settings tab

[English](047-failure-history-tab.md) | [繁體中文](047-failure-history-tab.zh-TW.md)

Status: planned, not implemented. Created: 2026-09-27. Queue after [046](046-countdown-sound.md) and before [035](035-guided-native-acceptance.md), which stays last. It has no hard dependency on 045 or 046; 046 also edits the settings model and panel, so running them in order avoids overlapping edits. 035's failure cases operate this UI, so this plan must finish before them. Execution order: see [queue](README.md#order-and-status).

## Purpose and boundary

Recording failures are prepended above the preferences in both Settings tabs, and every unread record is an open `<details>` ([renderer](../src/renderer/settings.ts)). After a failure, opening Settings for any reason first shows a long block of reasons, guidance, buttons and technical details, and the preferences start below it. Inside a row, the heading repeats "Recording failure" and the time repeats the summary line. The maintainer finds this hard to read (2026-09-27).

This plan moves the history into its own tab, shows it as a plain list of rows with a thin border for focus and removes the repeated lines. It also fixes the Traditional Chinese window title and makes each tab keep its own scroll position. It changes where and how the history is shown, not what it holds or how it is kept: retention, acknowledgement, removal, persistence and its retries, file rechecks, the focus rules, the tray badge and menu, notifications and recording locks stay as designed in [desktop](../docs/system-design/desktop.md#recording-failure-results). Diagnostics about a preference, such as an unavailable saved display or the last display failure, stay beside their control in the Recording tab.

Maintainer requests (2026-09-27):

- Recording failures get their own tab instead of appearing expanded above the preferences.
- The window title reads "RecordStuff - 設置" while the tray item reads "設定"; use 設定 for both.
- The maintainer asked whether a separate window would suit the failure history better than the Settings panel, and how Cap does it; see [Cap reference](#cap-reference) and [Tab or separate window](#tab-or-separate-window).
- (2026-09-28) Focus shows on the whole row's frame, not on the expand/collapse title, and the cards give way to plain rows separated by hairlines; see [Presentation and focus](#presentation-and-focus).
- (2026-09-28) Switching between the Recording and General tabs does not keep the scroll position; fix it here, since this plan rebuilds the tabs and adds a third.

## Cap reference

At pinned revision `119edf04864b59abfc0d52f60bf77d7f33cfd2b8` (static source review, Cap not run; upstream `20c224073bece3fbebed8acb631bd2df97cd6f40` changes none of these files):

- Settings is one window titled ["Cap Settings"](https://github.com/CapSoftware/Cap/blob/119edf04864b59abfc0d52f60bf77d7f33cfd2b8/apps/desktop/src-tauri/src/windows.rs#L1287-L1289) with a sidebar of thirteen pages, and the [tray](https://github.com/CapSoftware/Cap/blob/119edf04864b59abfc0d52f60bf77d7f33cfd2b8/apps/desktop/src-tauri/src/tray.rs#L591-L597) also says Settings. History and diagnostics are pages of that window beside the preferences: [Recordings, Screenshots, Feedback and Changelog share one flat list with General and Recording quality](https://github.com/CapSoftware/Cap/blob/119edf04864b59abfc0d52f60bf77d7f33cfd2b8/apps/desktop/src/routes/%28window-chrome%29/settings.tsx#L198-L264).
- A failed recording is a status on that recording, not a separate history: the Recordings page shows a ["Recording failed" pill whose error text appears only in a tooltip](https://github.com/CapSoftware/Cap/blob/119edf04864b59abfc0d52f60bf77d7f33cfd2b8/apps/desktop/src/routes/%28window-chrome%29/settings/recordings.tsx#L401-L419), in a flat list that never collapses.
- The tray's [View all recordings opens the Settings window directly on that page](https://github.com/CapSoftware/Cap/blob/119edf04864b59abfc0d52f60bf77d7f33cfd2b8/apps/desktop/src-tauri/src/tray.rs#L970-L987), separately from its Settings item.
- There is no in-app log viewer: [Settings → Feedback offers a diagnostic report and Upload Logs](https://github.com/CapSoftware/Cap/blob/119edf04864b59abfc0d52f60bf77d7f33cfd2b8/apps/desktop/src/routes/%28window-chrome%29/settings/feedback.tsx#L280-L398), and the tray has an Upload Logs item.

Adopted: the history as its own page inside the one Settings window, reached directly from the tray; one name, Settings, everywhere; no in-app log viewer. Not adopted: attaching failures to a recordings library (RecordStuff keeps a failure history, not a media library), error text only in a tooltip (guidance and actions must stay readable and keyboard-reachable), and a log upload (RecordStuff has no service to receive one; Show log keeps revealing the file).

## Tab or separate window

This plan implements a tab, as the maintainer first asked, and recommends it over a separate window:

- The reference app does the same: Cap keeps history and diagnostics as pages of its Settings window and links the tray straight to them, as RecordStuff's View recording failures and error notifications will.
- Failures and preferences are used together: guidance such as "Check your recording settings before trying again" leads to the Recording tab of the same window.
- A second window would need its own window lifecycle, preload, sender authorization, size persistence, close shortcut, crash handling and focus handling for a Dock-less app. Settings already has all of these, and they have been verified; the Settings fixture and 035's failure cases would also need rewriting.
- The actual log file stays behind Show log in the tray; no log viewer is added.

Revisit a separate window only if the history grows beyond failures, for example into a list of saved recordings; that would be a library, a separate product decision. If the maintainer prefers a separate window anyway, rewrite this plan's contract before implementing it.

## Presentation and focus

On 2026-09-28 the maintainer asked for two more changes. Keyboard focus lands on the expand/collapse title and draws its ring around that text; the maintainer wants the whole row's frame to show focus instead, with its own look. The maintainer also dislikes the warning-coloured cards and asked for something more compact and easier to read.

Three layouts were drafted with the app's colours, type and real Traditional Chinese strings, beside today's cards (static HTML, not repository artifacts):

- **A. Plain list grouped by day (chosen).** Rows sit directly on the window background under small day headings, separated by hairlines, with no card or container. Each row has two lines: the unread dot, the reason and the time, then the file outcome. Opening a row shows its details directly below it and closes the previously open row. (The first version of A put each day in an inset list like the other Settings sections; the maintainer asked for plain rows instead.)
- **B. One-line list with a detail pane.** Every failure is one line, and the selected one's details appear in a pane under the list. The list never moves, but two stacked panes are cramped at the 380 × 360 minimum, selection becomes a second state beside focus, and screen-reader users have to move between the list and the pane.
- **C. Log-style lines.** A time column and one short phrase per failure, the most compact. It needs a second, shorter set of reason and outcome strings in both languages, and the terse lines are harder to understand for someone who does not read logs.

A keeps the full reason text, fits the minimum window, shows at most one set of details at a time and stops repeating the date on every row. The maintainer chose A on 2026-09-28, and the contract below implements it.

Focus design (third draft, 2026-09-28). The first draft, a solid 2 px frame with a tint, looked like a web page; the second, a soft 3 px ring around an inset tint, was too heavy. The maintainer asked for focus drawn on the border alone:

- **Border only.** A focused row shows a thin accent border around the whole record, the header and any open details, with 6 px corners, and nothing else: no fill, no glow and no ring on the title text. The hairlines above and below it disappear so they do not run into the border.
- **Weight.** 1.5 px on Retina displays and 1 px on 1× displays, both about 0.3 mm, chosen by the maintainer over 1 px everywhere. The maintainer's primary display, a 27-inch 1920 × 1080 panel, is 1×, where a 1.5 px line would blur.
- **Focus inside a row.** When Tab moves into an open row's buttons, the row's border turns the neutral border grey at 1 px and the focused button's border turns accent at the focus weight.
- **Whole panel.** Every Settings control (tabs, segmented controls, switches, menus and buttons) uses the same weight in place of today's solid 2 px outline with its 2 px offset. On buttons and menus the line sits on the control's own border, so the border simply turns accent; accent-filled controls, such as a checked switch, the selected segment and Confirm, keep a 2 px gap so the line stays visible.
- **Other conditions.** Only keyboard focus shows it, following the existing pointer rule, and an inactive window shows none. Increased contrast uses a 2 px accent border; forced colours use a 2 px `Highlight` border. The chevron, pointing right, turns down over 150 ms when a row opens, at once with Reduce motion.

## Implementation contract

- [x] **Maintainer choice first.** Decided on 2026-09-28: layout A as a plain list, the third focus draft in [Presentation and focus](#presentation-and-focus), and the recommended weight of 1.5 px on Retina displays and 1 px on 1× displays.
- [ ] **Third tab.** The Settings view adds a Recording failures tab after General ("Recording failures", "失敗紀錄"). It is always present, so the tab strip never changes shape when the first failure arrives or the last row is removed. While failures are unread its label carries the count, such as "Recording failures (2)" or "失敗紀錄（2）", and its accessible name includes the count. Arrow keys, Home and End cover all three tabs. At the 380 px minimum width the three labels fit in both languages without wrapping or truncation; if they do not, shorten the labels (for example "Failures") instead of letting the strip wrap.
- [ ] **Preference tabs.** Recording and General no longer render the history. The hidden h1, the Recording tab's locked footnote and the General footer stay where they are.
- [ ] **The failures tab.** From top to bottom: the history-wide status line as today (loading, or a history that cannot be read or saved), the rows newest first with their own save warnings and Retry saving reminder, and the retention note ("Keeps all unreviewed failures and the 20 most recently reviewed failures…") as a footnote. With no failures it shows a short empty state ("No recording failures." / "沒有失敗紀錄。") instead of an empty list; while loading it shows the existing loading line.
- [ ] **Plain list.** Rows are grouped under day headings (Today, Yesterday, then the date, with the year only when it is not the current year) and sit directly on the window background, with no inset list or card, separated by hairlines that stop 10 px short of each side; the warning-coloured card is removed, and warning colour is kept for save warnings and action errors. Main's view supplies each row's day heading and its short local time, as it formats the time today. A row's header has two lines: the unread marker (a dot plus a bold title, with "Unread" in its accessible name, so it is not colour alone and stays visible in forced colours), the reason and the time with a chevron; then the file outcome in secondary text. Every row starts collapsed, and one row is open at a time: opening another closes it, while a pending save or action keeps its state by id. The details, indented under the title, no longer repeat the "Recording failure" heading or the time: the guidance, the file name (the full path moves into Technical details), the actions with Got it at the end of the row and Remove from history only on reviewed rows, the saving and error lines, and Technical details as a text disclosure. Got it still collapses its row.
- [ ] **Row focus.** The row header is the focus target: a button with `aria-expanded` (or the existing `summary`). Keyboard focus draws the thin accent border described in [Presentation and focus](#presentation-and-focus) around the whole record, never an outline on the title text, and hides the two neighbouring hairlines; focus on a button inside an open row turns the row's border neutral grey. The chevron turns over 150 ms, at once with Reduce motion. Up and Down move between row headers across day groups, Home and End go to the first and last, Enter and Space open or close, and Tab still reaches every header and the open row's controls. The existing focus rules keep their intent with the header as the target: after Got it, collapse or a failed action focus returns to that row's header; after removing the last row it goes to the Recording failures tab; it never moves when the user moved it or the window lost focus meanwhile.
- [ ] **Shared focus border.** [settings.css](../src/renderer/settings.css) defines the focus weight once (1.5 px at a device pixel ratio of 2 or more, 1 px below it, and 2 px for increased contrast and forced colours) and uses it for the failure rows and every Settings control in place of the solid 2 px outline with its 2 px offset: on buttons and menus the line sits on the control's border, and accent-filled controls keep a 2 px gap. Pointer input still hides it. If Chromium keeps `:focus-visible` after the window loses focus, hide it from the page's window `blur` and `focus` events, so an inactive window shows none.
- [ ] **Entry points.** View recording failures in the tray and an error notification select the Recording failures tab, open only the newest unread row (the newest row when all are reviewed), scroll it into view and focus its header, without acknowledging it: today's focus target, now in its own tab. The Settings menu item and the Settings shortcut keep today's behaviour: a new window opens on Recording and an open window keeps its selected tab. Removing the last row moves focus to the Recording failures tab, as the existing rule sends it to the active tab.
- [ ] **Window title.** The Traditional Chinese translation of "RecordStuff - Settings" becomes "RecordStuff - 設定"; the English title is unchanged and the title does not change with the tab. No other Traditional Chinese string uses 設置. Update the settings-model test and the Settings fixture, which hard-codes the old title.
- [ ] **Scroll position per tab.** Today switching tabs rebuilds the panel ([renderer](../src/renderer/settings.ts)) and carries one saved `scrollTop` across it, so the tab being opened receives the previous tab's offset, clamped when it is shorter, and returning to a tab does not bring back where it was left. The saved offset is also applied before the failure section is added back at the top of the new panel. Each tab keeps its own scroll position for the life of the window instead: switching away stores it, switching back restores it once the panel, including any failure rows, is complete, and a tab opened for the first time starts at the top. A background update within the same tab keeps its position, as today. An entry from View recording failures or an error notification still scrolls to its target row, overriding the stored position, and a new window starts every tab at the top. Keep the rebuild with a stored offset per tab, or render one panel per tab and toggle it, whichever the three-tab change makes simpler; the behaviour is the contract.
- [ ] **Settings fixture.** `pnpm acceptance:settings` reaches failure rows through the new tab. Add cases, with real keyboard and mouse input: no history in the Recording and General tabs; the label count appearing and clearing as rows are acknowledged; result entry selecting the tab and opening only its target; rows collapsed on a normal open and only one open at a time; Up, Down, Home and End between headers across day groups, and Enter and Space toggling; the row's accent border on keyboard focus, the grey row border with the button's accent border after Tab into the actions, the hidden neighbouring hairlines, no border after a click, and none while the window is inactive; the empty state after removing the last row, with focus on the tab; keyboard navigation across three tabs; each tab's scroll position surviving a switch away and back with real scrolling, a first visit starting at the top, and a result entry scrolling to its row; and screenshots of the tab strip, the row focus states and the shared focus border on each kind of control, including a checked switch and the selected segment, at the minimum size in both languages, light and dark.
- [ ] **Documentation.** Update in both languages: the desktop design's Settings window paragraphs (three tabs, the title) and Recording failure results ("sits above preferences in either Settings tab", "each independently expandable record", the day groups, one open row, row focus and entry behaviour), the Settings window's focus paragraph ("restores the 2px focus line") and its note that background pushes keep the scroll position (each tab now keeps its own), the acceptance guide's failure-result rows, and the [controlled acceptance build](../docs/system-design/tooling.md#controlled-acceptance-build) instructions if they say where the history appears. Record the tab-or-window decision and its reason in [design decisions](../docs/system-design/decisions.md).

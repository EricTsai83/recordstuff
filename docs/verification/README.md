## Finder foreground after a notification click — 2026-09-20

Plan 014 reproduced the v0.1.0 report on the installed 0.1.2 and macOS 26.6.2 with the new unattended check `pnpm acceptance:notification` ([tooling](../system-design/tooling.md#notification-acceptance)): the "Saved …" banner is pressed through Accessibility while a fresh TextEdit window is in front, and the frontmost app is sampled for 3 s. The app log with `did-become-active` instrumentation gave the mechanism: macOS activates the notifying app about 110 ms after the click callback, and only for the copy registered in `/Applications`; when that activation lands after Finder's, the windowless app stays frontmost and Finder keeps the file selected behind the user's window. It rarely hit the first click of a process. Unfixed 0.1.2, six clicks with Finder closed: two clicks left RecordStuff frontmost (1756 run), and a later five-click run of the same build failed three times including the first click (1818 run, one of them an undelivered click); the instrumented build showed the same two of six with `did-become-active` logged 4 ms after each failing reveal (1748 run).

The fix (a one-shot `did-become-active` listener for 1 s after the reveal that reveals again from the now-active app, [desktop](../system-design/desktop.md#tray-and-notifications)) was installed into `/Applications` for the run and judged with `--full` over 5 clicks × 3 Finder states × 2 languages. Of 30 planned clicks (35 recordings with retries; 1759 run), 26 passed, 3 could not be judged because Notification Center showed no banner for that save, and 1 failed in a different way: the banner was pressed and the app was activated, but no click reached the app (no reveal in the log), so Finder was never asked; the unfixed build showed the same undelivered click once in the 1756 run. In 13 of the 26 passing clicks the log shows `reveal repeated after activation`, i.e. macOS did activate the app after the first reveal and the second reveal put Finder back in front within about 0.4 s; no delivered click ended with RecordStuff frontmost. Finder selection and foreground are recorded per click in the report; the English and Traditional Chinese banner bodies matched. Not covered: the tray menu's own reveal (Accessibility cannot reach the tray, see the 016 notes), other Spaces, and clicks from the Notification Center list after the banner has left; the fixed bytes are not yet published.

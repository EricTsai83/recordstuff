## Goal and current state

The Recording Studio's preview is one H.264 stream of the composed picture, 640 pixels wide (`PREVIEW_WIDTH`, [camera_layout.rs](../native/capture/src/camera_layout.rs)) at 1 Mbps, shown at 10 fps while someone works in the Studio and 5 while it is only looked at ([studio-pace.ts](../src/renderer/settings/tabs/studio-pace.ts), plan 081's D1). "Works in" is the pointer over the window or a key, click or scroll within 3 s, not window focus. The page sets the rate on the Studio's port for whatever session it shows, a Live preview or a recording. The maintainer finds the 5 fps rate slow and wants the rate to be the user's choice; the user also cannot see the screen without the camera, only the composed picture.

The width of 640 is the stream's resolution, not the size on the page: the stage is at most 220 CSS pixels tall at the display's shape (about 391 × 220 at 16:9, [ui.css](../src/renderer/ui.css)), and stays that size whatever is chosen here. Neither the preview's resolution nor the camera's capture size (1280 × 720 on both platforms) becomes a setting: at this stage size a larger preview is not seen, and a different camera size brings no performance gain (maintainer, 2026-10-23).

## Decisions (maintainer, 2026-10-23, revised the same day)

- **D1 — a preview rate choice** in the Studio, above the stage: **Off** (**the default**: no preview is composed, encoded or drawn, and the Studio opens no camera of its own), **Power saving** (5 fps while only looked at, 10 while someone works in it: today's behavior under a name that says what it does), **Standard** (10 fps always) and **Smooth** (15 fps always, subject to D2). Each option carries a one-line description; with Power saving chosen, a small label in the stage's corner shows the rate in use and why (for example `10 fps · working` / `5 fps · saving`). Off keeps the stage's frame and shape with "Preview is off" and a button that turns the preview on at the last rate the user chose (Power saving the first time); the bubble's outline still drags over the empty stage and the meters still run (their subscription is separate). Before a session, Off also means the page does not open the camera with `getUserMedia` for the bubble ([camera-preview.tsx](../src/renderer/settings/tabs/camera-preview.tsx)), so opening the Studio never turns on the camera light. The choice is one persisted setting, the same for a Live preview and a recording: a user who turns the preview on keeps it on until they turn it off. The default was Standard when first planned, then Power saving; the maintainer set it to Off on 2026-10-23, so opening the Studio costs nothing over a recording (D18 holds by default) and the preview is something the user asks for.
- **D2 — the costs of Power saving (while worked in), Standard and Smooth are accepted over D18 as opt-in choices,** measured, not estimated. 15 fps is estimated at about 15 points over a 30 fps recording; on a stage about 391 × 220 its visible gain over 10 fps is small. Smooth ships only if its measured share is at most about 4 points over Standard's; otherwise it is removed from D1 before closure and the finding recorded. The closure records the figures and updates the budget's text in [decisions](../docs/system-design/decisions.md).
- **D3 — a view choice: Composed / Screen**, as segments beside the rate. The stage keeps its size and the recording's shape. Screen draws the screen without the camera; the outline still drags. Only the preview changes: the recording is always the composed picture. The Camera view (the camera alone, enlarged) is [094](094-studio-camera-view.md); the `view` message is designed here so 094 adds a value, not a message.

## Steps and ownership

- [ ] **Baseline before.** On 091's final bundle (or the current HEAD if 091 adopted nothing), measure the Studio as it is: S (watched, 5 fps) and S with `--studio-active` (10 fps) and L at 30 and 60 fps, `--repeat 3 --diagnostics`, and R in the same round, since the Studio's figures are shares over it. Count the preview thread's wake-ups in the capture process from the profile.
- [ ] **The rate on the engine side.** The preview thread schedules its next frame at the chosen rate's interval instead of ticking at `PREVIEW_FPS` and skipping ticks, so it wakes only when it makes a frame (5, 10 or 15 times a second); a rate or view change wakes it at once and restarts its schedule, and `preview_tick_shows` is replaced by that schedule. The encoder's configured rate and the 1 Mbps bit rate are checked at 15 fps. The port's `rate` message (`isStudioRate`, [studio-port.ts](../src/shared/studio-port.ts)) accepts the rates of D1; Off is the page no longer watching the preview (the path used when the stage leaves sight), not a rate of 0. macOS and Windows sessions both.
- [ ] **The rate on the page side.** A setting in main's Studio model ([model/studio.ts](../src/main/settings/model/studio.ts)), persisted, default Off, English and Traditional Chinese in i18n. `paceStudio` runs only under Power saving; the other choices send one fixed rate; the corner label reads what `paceStudio` shows. Unit tests for the choice, its default and persistence, and the pacing per option.
- [ ] **The Screen view.** A `view` message on the Studio's port (`composed` or `screen`; unknown values refused by its validator); the compositor's preview render takes the view: Screen is today's path with the camera hidden (D13). A change of view forces a key frame. The recording's `compose` is untouched. Rust tests for both platforms' compositors that the preview's parameters follow the view and the recording's do not. The page: the segments, and before a session the Screen view says it appears with Live preview. Production components and CSS only, per the [UI alignment rules](../docs/testing.md#keep-test-ui-aligned-with-the-app).
- [ ] **Measure after and document.** The same cases as the baseline at each rate and Off, and with someone working in it under Power saving; a recording with the camera switched between views, its file checked to be the composed picture. Update the Studio's sections in [recording](../docs/system-design/recording.md), [decisions](../docs/system-design/decisions.md) and the tooling guide's [pipeline baseline](../docs/system-design/tooling.md#pipeline-baseline) and budget (the Studio rows at every rate become the baseline 084 and 094 measure against), with translations.

## Gates and completion

Off, the default, costs nothing over R: the Studio watched with the preview off is within R's spread and opens no camera. Power saving costs what today's Studio does: CPU and the capture process's wake-ups within the baseline's spread at both rates, measured back to back with the baseline per the [testing policy](../docs/testing.md#keep-recording-rounds-short), since the difference is small. Each rate shows the chosen number of frames a second, measured from the page's drawn count; Power saving goes to 10 within one tick of work and back to 5 after 3 s; Off composes, encodes and draws nothing while the meters continue. Smooth is kept or removed by D2's rule. Screen shows the screen without the camera, and switching views never changes the recorded file. A rate or view change never fails or stalls a recording.

# Rules for working on plans

[English](RULES.md) | [繁體中文](RULES.zh-TW.md)

These rules apply to every agent and person executing or updating a plan in this folder, alongside the [plan index](README.md). Added on 2026-10-29 at the maintainer's request.

## Replies and questions stand on their own

Every reply, progress update, question and decision request to the maintainer must be understandable without reading the plan:

- Give enough information to act on. Say what the question is about, why it matters, what each option means in practice and what it costs, in plain words.
- Do not lean on the plan's internal labels. Gate, decision, requirement and principle codes (for example G1, D1, R2, P5), step numbers and stop-rule names may appear only next to a plain explanation of what they are, never in their place.
- Do not refer the maintainer to the plan, an earlier message or a file to understand what is being asked. If something earlier matters, restate it briefly.
- Each question or reply is read on its own: assume the maintainer sees only it, for instance in a question dialog without the conversation behind it.
